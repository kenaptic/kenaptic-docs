# Measurement

The **Monitor** group reports whether published cross-links are working: how readers use them, how
the estate changes over time, and a complete record of every action taken.

## Link Performance

**Link Performance** shows how published cross-links are used: which links get clicked, and where
they lead readers. Use it to identify the links readers follow to their next answer and the topics
that need more links.

The report measures reader behaviour rather than publication counts: it can show, for example,
that specific links route readers from docs pages to the forum threads that resolve their issue.

## Analytics

**Analytics** shows trends over time: how the estate's connectedness, activity, and reach change
as you work the review loop. Use it to confirm sustained progress and to spot metrics that need
attention.

## Reports

**Reports** generates shareable periodic summaries of what Kenaptic has done and the difference it
made. Use them in place of dashboard screenshots for stakeholders who do not work in the app, such
as budget owners reviewing the return.

## Audit

**Audit** is the complete record of activity and decisions: who approved which link, when content
was published or retracted, and what changed. It serves two purposes:

- Traceability: the record shows why any given link exists, with a precise history.
- Compliance: the trail is available when you, your legal team, or a customer's process requires
  it.

Every action Kenaptic takes is recorded and reviewable after the fact.
