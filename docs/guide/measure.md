# Measure the impact

The **Monitor** group answers the question that matters most: *is this working?* Kenaptic is built
so you never have to take that on faith.

## Link Performance

**Link Performance** shows how the cross-links you've published are actually being used — which
links get clicked, and where they lead readers. That tells you which connections are genuinely
helping people find their next answer, and which topics are hungriest for more links.

It's the difference between "we added 300 links" and "these links are sending readers from the docs
to the exact forum threads that solve their problem."

## Analytics

**Analytics** zooms out to trends over time: how your estate's connectedness, activity, and reach
are changing as you work the review loop. Use it to see momentum — the estate getting steadily
healthier — and to spot when something needs attention.

## Reports

**Reports** turns all of this into shareable summaries you can hand to stakeholders. Instead of
screenshotting dashboards, you get a clean, periodic summary of what Kenaptic has done and the
difference it's making — useful for the people who approved the budget and want to see the return.

## Audit

**Audit** is the complete record of activity and decisions: who approved which link, when content
was published or retracted, and what changed. It exists for two reasons:

- **Peace of mind** — you can always answer "why is this link here?" with a precise history.
- **Compliance** — the trail is there when you, your legal team, or a customer's process needs it.

Everything Kenaptic does is accountable and reviewable after the fact.
