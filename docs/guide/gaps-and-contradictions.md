# Gaps and contradictions

Kenaptic analyses your whole estate at once, so it can detect two kinds of problem beyond missing
links: **missing content** and **conflicting content**.

## Content Gaps

A **content gap** is a question your audience is asking where your coverage is thin or missing.
Kenaptic finds gaps by comparing silos: for example, a topic your forum is full of questions
about that your documentation barely covers.

On the **Content Gaps** page you can:

- See gaps grouped by topic, so related items sit together.
- Inspect the evidence for each gap.
- Start closing a gap from the page, and mark it resolved once it is covered.

Gaps tell you what to write next, backed by what your audience is asking.

## Contradictions

A **contradiction** is where two of your own pages say different things about the same subject.
A common case: your documentation still describes how something used to work, while a blog post
has already announced the change. Both pages are live, and a reader can land on either.

The **Contradictions** page:

- Shows both pages side by side, with the conflicting claims highlighted.
- Gives a confidence indicator, so you can prioritise the clearest conflicts first.
- Lets you fix the conflict in place. You can edit either page to reconcile the two. For sources
  Kenaptic can write to, that fix goes into the same review-and-publish flow as a cross-link. For
  a source Kenaptic only reads, the conflict is flagged for you to resolve at the source.
- Lets you set a contradiction aside if it is expected or already being handled. Set-aside items
  move to their own category and stay on record.

Contradiction detection catches places where your content conflicts with itself, so statements
stay consistent across every silo.
