# The review loop

Everything in Kenaptic comes back to one repeatable loop. Once you've done it a few times, the
whole product will feel familiar.

<div class="grid" markdown>

**1. Kenaptic proposes** → **2. You review** → **3. Kenaptic publishes** → **4. Kenaptic keeps it fresh**

</div>

## 1. Kenaptic proposes

After your sources are connected, Kenaptic fills the **Cross-link Review** queue with proposed
links. Each one names the two pages it would connect, the relationship between them, and a
confidence indicator to help you prioritise.

## 2. You review

Open **Cross-link Review** and work through the queue. For each proposal you can:

- **Approve** it as-is.
- **Edit** it — for example, adjust the wording of the link — then approve.
- **Reject** it if it isn't right.

You can preview the exact change before deciding. Approving a link doesn't publish it yet; it moves
it to the publish step, so you can review in a batch and publish when you're ready.

!!! tip "You don't have to review everything at once"
    Sort by confidence and start with the strongest proposals. Approving a handful of clearly-good
    links on day one is more valuable than trying to clear the whole queue.

## 3. Kenaptic publishes

Approved links are published from the **Link Injection** page. Kenaptic writes each link **into the
page's own source**, so it's a real, native link that works for every reader and crawler. Links are
clearly marked as Kenaptic's and can be removed cleanly at any time.

## 4. Kenaptic keeps it fresh

Kenaptic re-checks your estate on a schedule. As content changes, it proposes new links and flags
ones that no longer make sense — which brings you right back to the review queue. The loop keeps
your cross-links current without anyone having to remember to maintain them.

## Where the rest of the product fits

The loop above is the spine. The other pages support it:

- **Content Gaps** and **Contradictions** surface deeper issues to fix, not just links to add.
- **AI Readiness** tells you how well machines can understand your content.
- **Link Performance**, **Analytics**, and **Reports** show the loop is paying off.
- **Audit** keeps the record of every decision.

The [**Using Kenaptic**](../guide/discover.md) section walks through each of these in turn.

## Don't watch the queue — let it tell you

You don't need to keep checking whether there's anything to review. Kenaptic can post to Slack, Google Chat, Microsoft Teams or email whenever links are waiting, with a link straight to the queue. See [Cross-link notifications](../guide/notifications.md).
