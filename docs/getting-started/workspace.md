# Your workspace

Your Kenaptic workspace is where your whole team connects sources, reviews proposed links, and
tracks impact. This page covers signing in and finding your way around.

## Signing in

Kenaptic uses a secure sign-in with **two-factor authentication (2FA)**. The first time you sign
in, you'll be asked to set up an authenticator app (such as Google Authenticator, 1Password, or
Authy). After that, each sign-in asks for your password and a six-digit code from that app.

!!! tip "Seats are unlimited"
    Invite your whole documentation, support, and web team. Kenaptic is priced on the size of the
    content estate you keep connected — not on the number of people reviewing it. More reviewers
    means a healthier estate, so bring everyone in.

## The layout

The app is organised into three groups in the left-hand menu, which follow the natural order of
work:

| Group | What it's for | Pages |
|---|---|---|
| **Discover** | Understand your estate and find opportunities | Overview, Domains, Documents, Knowledge Graph, Content Gaps, Contradictions |
| **Connect** | Review and publish the links | Cross-link Review, Link Injection, AI Readiness |
| **Monitor** | See what's working | Link Performance, Analytics, Reports, Audit, Settings |

You'll spend most of your first week in **Discover** (connecting sources and letting Kenaptic map
them) and **Connect** (reviewing and publishing links). **Monitor** becomes your home once links
are live.

## The overview dashboard

The **Overview** page is your landing spot. It summarises the health of your estate — how much
content is connected, how many links are live, what's new since last time, and anything that needs
your attention. Use it as your daily starting point.

## Working in more than one workspace

If you belong to several workspaces, **Switch workspace** appears at the foot of the sidebar
with all of them listed; the one you are in is marked and is not a link. Each workspace is a
separate estate with its own crawl, its own proposals and its own database — switching moves
you between them, it does not merge them.

If you only belong to one, the control is hidden, because there is nowhere to switch to.
